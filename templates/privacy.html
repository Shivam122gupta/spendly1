Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you. Here's exactly how we handle it.

Last updated: May 2025

01

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account and use the Service, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password provided at registration.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected to maintain and improve the Service.

We do not collect payment card details, government ID numbers, or any sensitive financial account credentials.

02

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to provide, maintain, and improve your experience with Spendly. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Display your expense records and summaries within the app.
  • Send important service-related communications (e.g. password resets).
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We will never use your data to serve targeted advertisements, nor will we sell or rent it to any third party for marketing purposes.

03

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers and protected by industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed password storage. Access to production data is strictly limited to authorised personnel.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to log out of Spendly when using shared or public devices.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

04

Third-Party Services

Spendly may use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the platform (for example, hosting providers). These partners are bound by data processing agreements and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

The Service does not integrate with any external financial institutions, payment processors, or advertising networks. We do not embed third-party tracking scripts or analytics that share your data outside the Spendly platform.

05

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. You also have the right to object to or restrict certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the section below. We will respond to all legitimate requests within a reasonable timeframe.

06

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please reach out through the contact information available on the Spendly homepage. We take all privacy enquiries seriously and will do our best to respond promptly.
